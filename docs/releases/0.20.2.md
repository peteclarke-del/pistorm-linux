## Fixed

- **iGame now opens.** It stopped before showing its window, because cards were given MUI 3.8 from 1997. Machines with a 68020 or better, including every PiStorm, now get MUI 5, the current release, which is free from its developers. A stock 68000 keeps MUI 3.8, because MUI 5 needs a 68020.

## Tested in FS-UAE

All tested on an AmigaOS 3.2 A1200 card with a 68040 and 256 MB:

- **iGame:** opens.
- **NetSurf:** loads its welcome page.
- **AGS2:** shows its games menu.

If you built a card with MUI programs using an earlier version, rebuild its System drive to get MUI 5.
