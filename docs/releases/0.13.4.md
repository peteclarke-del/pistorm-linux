A fix for CF cards and IDE drives on an A600 or A1200: drives past the first 4 GB of the card came up as not formatted.

## What was wrong

Before Kickstart 3.1.4, the A600's and A1200's built-in driver for the IDE port reaches only the first 4 GB of a drive. AmigaOS 3.2 brings a driver that reaches the rest, and loads it at startup on an older Kickstart. Two things stopped that from working:

- AmigaOS 3.2 looks for that driver in a drawer named for the machine it is running on. A card built for an A1200 had only the A1200's copy, so in an A600 the old driver stayed and every drive past 4 GB was lost.
- The new driver is loaded from the bootable drive, so the old driver has to be able to read that drive first. A bootable drive larger than 4 GB could not be started on an older Kickstart.

## What changes

- A drive built for an A600 or an A1200 now carries the driver under both names. The two machines use the same file, so a card works in either.
- A drive that relies on its machine's own Kickstart is refused if its bootable drive reaches past the first 4 GB.
- A drive installed from the Workbench disks is refused if any of it lies past 4 GB, because those disks have no driver that can reach it.
- Behind a PiStorm with a 3.2 Kickstart, the Kickstart's own driver reaches the whole card, and neither limit applies.

The build log says which of these applies to each drive.
